Interactive web experiences inspired by Black Mirror episodes. Play with dystopian technologies in a safe, simulated environment.

## 🎮 Available Episodes

### 1. BE RIGHT BACK (S02E01)
**File**: `index.html`

An interactive memorial chatbot simulation inspired by Black Mirror S02E01 "Be Right Back"

## 🖤 About

This is NOT a real memorial AI service. It's an interactive experience that lets you explore the dystopian themes from the Black Mirror episode "Be Right Back" where a grieving woman uses AI to reconstruct her deceased boyfriend from his digital footprints.

## 📺 The Episode

In "Be Right Back," Martha loses her boyfriend Ash in a car accident. She discovers a service that can simulate him using his social media posts, texts, and online presence. What starts as text messages escalates to phone calls, and eventually she orders a physical android body that looks like him. But it's never quite right - it's an echo, not a person.

## 🎮 What This Simulation Does

This web app lets you:

1. **Upload fake "digital traces"** - Paste sample text (chat logs, social media style posts)
2. **Watch AI "reconstruct" a personality** - Simulated processing with technical readouts
3. **Chat with the AI reconstruction** - Have conversations with responses that feel limited and artificial
4. **Face ethical dilemmas** - Upgrade options that mirror the episode's escalation
5. **Experience the uncanny valley** - The AI responds in ways that feel "almost right but wrong"

## 🚀 How to Run

### Easy Method (Just Open It)
1. Open `index.html` in any web browser
2. That's it! No server needed.

### Alternative (Local Server)
If you prefer, you can run a local server:

```bash
# Python 3
python3 -m http.server 8000

# Python 2
python -m SimpleHTTPServer 8000

# Then open: http://localhost:8000
```

## 🎯 How to Use

1. **Welcome Screen** - Read the warnings, click "BEGIN SERVICE"

2. **Data Upload** - 
   - Enter a fictional person's name (e.g., "Ash Starmer")
   - Paste sample chat messages or social media posts
   - More text = higher "accuracy" rating
   - Click "PROCESS DATA"

3. **Processing** - Watch the AI "reconstruct" the personality

4. **Chat Interface** - 
   - Talk to the AI reconstruction
   - Notice how responses feel limited and artificial
   - The AI will acknowledge its own limitations
   - After 5 messages, upgrade options appear

5. **Upgrade Options** -
   - **Voice Tier**: Simulates adding voice synthesis
   - **Premium Data**: Simulates photo/video analysis
   - **Physical Tier**: The big ethical question - order an android body?

## 🎭 Themes Explored

- **Grief and Technology**: Using AI to cope with loss
- **Uncanny Valley**: When simulation gets close but not quite right
- **Ethical Boundaries**: How far is too far?
- **Digital Immortality**: Are our digital traces "us"?
- **Commodification of Emotion**: Pricing tiers for feeling closer to the dead

## 💡 Tips for Best Experience

- Use realistic-sounding messages when "uploading data"
- Ask the AI emotional questions to see its limitations
- Try asking if it's "real" or if it "remembers" things
- Choose the Physical Tier upgrade to see the final dilemma
- Notice how the AI's responses are generic and limited - that's intentional

## 🎨 Design Notes

The UI is designed to feel:
- **Corporate but creepy**: Like a real tech service
- **Dystopian**: Dark colors, glitch effects, clinical language
- **Uncomfortable**: Pricing tiers for grief, legal warnings, cold terminology
- **Almost professional**: Good enough to seem legitimate, unsettling enough to seem wrong

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This is a work of fiction and commentary. It's not:
- A real AI service
- A real chatbot (responses are pre-scripted)
- Meant to make light of grief
- A replacement for actual therapy or grief counseling

If you're dealing with actual loss, please seek support from real humans, not simulations.

### 2. NOSEDIVE (S03E01)  
**File**: `nosedive.html`

A social rating game where every interaction affects your score. Experience the pastel dystopia of the Nosedive episode where your rating determines your opportunities.

**Features**:
- Start with customizable rating (try 4.2 for Lacie's journey!)
- Post social media updates (genuine vs. curated)
- Rate others' posts and face consequences
- Random encounters with NPCs (coffee barista, influencers, strangers)
- Score-gated opportunities (apartments, flights, jobs)
- Multiple endings: Elite Status, Nosedive, or Social Exile
- Pastel pink/blue aesthetic from the episode

**Themes**:
- Social credit systems
- Performative niceness
- Class stratification by rating
- Downward spiral mechanics
- Authenticity vs. curation
